Mail Intelligence Personal

Mail Intelligence Personal is a private, internal tool operated by Ashtar Ecommerce. It produces on-demand summary reports of email activity for mailboxes that the operator owns or manages on behalf of its group companies.

It is not offered to the public and cannot be signed up for.

  • Access is read-only (Gmail scope gmail.readonly). It never sends, deletes, archives or modifies email.
  • It runs on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.
