Privacy Policy — Mail Intelligence Personal

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

Mail Intelligence Personal ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Ashtar Ecommerce for mailboxes it owns or manages. It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the account holder's explicit Google OAuth consent, the app uses the read-only Gmail scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) to read message metadata and content for the period requested in a report. It does not request permission to send, delete or modify email, and it never asks for passwords.

How the data is used

Email data is used only to produce summary reports for the operator (for example orders, deliveries, refunds, invoices, subscriptions and outstanding actions). Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except as described below.

Processing and storage

  • The app runs on the operator's own computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and protected by the operating system's credential store.
  • To write reports, condensed excerpts of relevant emails are processed by an AI assistant (Anthropic Claude) under the operator's own account.
  • Generated reports are saved on the operator's computer. Full mailboxes are not copied or exported.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections or by the operator disconnecting the account, which deletes its stored token. Reports are kept only as long as the operator needs them.
